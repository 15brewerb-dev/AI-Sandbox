Keep urgent HVAC requests visible. Keep routine follow-up organized.

ComfortRelay is an early-stage managed workflow for Columbus-area HVAC companies. It helps separate urgent inquiries from routine requests and keeps the next follow-up step visible without adding another complicated system for the team.

What ComfortRelay does

1. Capture
New inquiries are logged in a simple shared workflow.
2. Separate
Urgent requests are surfaced separately from routine service and estimate inquiries.
3. Follow up
Routine requests stay visible with a clear next action instead of relying on memory.

FAQ

Do we need new software?
Not necessarily. The pilot is designed to work around the tools already in use wherever practical.
What counts as urgent?
Urgency rules are agreed with the HVAC company before launch and can be adjusted during the pilot.
What if the workflow fails?
The pilot includes a documented failure-handling process and human review for consequential communication.
What happens after 30 days?
We review what worked, what did not, and whether it makes sense to continue. There is no automatic long-term commitment.
