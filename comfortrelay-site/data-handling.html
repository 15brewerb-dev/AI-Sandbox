ComfortRelay Data Handling

This page describes the intended data-handling procedure for early ComfortRelay pilots. It is operational guidance, not a legal compliance certification.

Data we may use

Only information needed to route and follow up on HVAC inquiries, such as name, contact details, inquiry text, urgency indicators, status, and follow-up notes.

Data we do not need

Payment card numbers, government IDs, medical records, passwords, or other unrelated sensitive information should not be entered into the workflow.

Access

Access is limited to the agreed pilot workflow and the people required to operate it. Access should be removed when it is no longer needed.

Corrections, exports, and deletion

During an early pilot, requests to correct, export, or delete pilot data are handled manually. At pilot end, the customer can request an export or deletion of data held specifically for the pilot, subject to any records that must be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Incidents

If a routing, notification, or access problem occurs, ComfortRelay pauses the affected workflow as needed, documents the issue, and contacts the customer before resuming consequential automation.
