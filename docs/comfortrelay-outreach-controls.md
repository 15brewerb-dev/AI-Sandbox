# ComfortRelay Outreach Controls

## Prospect sources
Use only:
- customer-provided contacts,
- publicly listed business contact information,
- or contacts otherwise permissioned for business outreach.

Record the source URL or source type and the date collected.

Do not buy or use restricted/private contact data without review.

## Opt-out / suppression
If a prospect asks not to be contacted again:
1. record the opt-out,
2. mark the contact suppressed,
3. prevent future outreach to that address,
4. do not resend until the suppression is intentionally and lawfully cleared.

## Message approval
The current operator, Brandon, approves the outbound template before use.
Record:
- template version,
- approval date,
- who approved it,
- material changes.

Any material change to pricing, claims, offer, or customer-facing commitments triggers re-approval.

## Complaint / compliance stop
Pause outreach immediately if there is a complaint, legal/compliance concern, unexpected platform warning, or evidence that the contact source is inappropriate. Review before resuming.
