### Definitions

- **Qualified conversation:** Target buyer confirms the problem, has a plausible budget, and agrees the proposed outcome matters.
- **Paid pilot/customer:** Money is collected, not merely promised.
- **Evidence quality:**
  - 1 = opinion or personal intuition
  - 2 = likes, clicks, or vague interest
  - 3 = relevant buyer conversation and specific pain
  - 4 = repeated pain plus commitment, referral, or access to data/workflow
  - 5 = payment, renewal, or multiple paying customers
- **Distribution traction:**
  - 1 = no reliable channel
  - 2 = manual outreach only with weak response
  - 3 = repeatable access and ≥10% response rate
  - 4 = referrals, inbound, partnerships, or ≥20% response rate
  - 5 = predictable acquisition at acceptable cost

# DEFAULT BUDGETS

These are **maximums, not spending targets**.

| Stage | Purpose | Default cash/API/software cap | Time cap |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Research | Customer/problem discovery, list building, outreach setup | **$20** | 4 hours |
| Prototype/demo | Manual mockup, sample output, lightweight workflow | **$40** | 8 hours |
| Validation | Paid pilot, delivery, and conversion test | **$60** | 12 hours |
| Contingency/reserve | Protected runway | **$80** | — |

**Total default allocation: $200.**

No experiment may automatically consume the reserve. Unused budget from a killed experiment returns to the reserve.

Spending must be incremental: research first, then prototype, then validation. Do not build a full product before payment evidence.

# KILL RULES

Kill or pause the experiment immediately when any of these apply:

1. **Zero replies after 30 targeted prospects** or after two materially different messages to the same audience.
2. **Fewer than 3 replies from 40 targeted prospects** unless at least one reply is a highly qualified buying signal.
3. **Zero qualified conversations after 5 replies** or 10 conversations.
4. **No demo request or concrete next step after 3 qualified conversations.**
5. **No paid pilot after 5 qualified conversations or 3 demos**, unless the offer, audience, or price is being explicitly renegotiated.
6. **No payment commitment after spending the approved stage budget.**
7. **Gross profit is negative** or expected gross margin is below 40% after one delivery cycle.
8. **Fulfillment requires more than 8 hours per customer**, recurring custom work, or undocumented manual intervention.
9. **Fulfillment difficulty is 4–5/5** without a credible simplification plan.
10. **Evidence quality remains 1–2/5** after the research stage.
11. The test depends on unverified assumptions and the team cannot state what evidence would change the decision.
12. Time or spend exceeds the approved cap by more than 20% without approval.

Time already spent is **sunk cost** and is not a reason to continue.

# CONTINUE RULES

Continue only when the experiment produces enough evidence for a specific next test:

- At least **30 targeted prospects reached**.
- At least **3 replies or a 10% reply rate**.
- At least **2 qualified conversations**.
- At least **one concrete buying signal**: requested demo, proposal, trial, data access, referral, or price discussion.
- Evidence quality is **≥3/5**.
- Fulfillment is feasible at **≤4 hours per customer**.
- Expected gross margin is **≥60%**, or there is a documented path to reach it.
- Spend is within the current stage cap.
- The next experiment has one clear change: audience, message, offer, price, channel, or delivery method.

A continuation gets only the **next stage’s budget**, not the full remaining runway.

# SCALE RULES

Scale only after real payment evidence:

1. At least **2 paying customers or paid pilots**, or one customer with a meaningful repeat/renewal.
2. At least **$200 collected revenue** or a documented path to that amount within the next two sales.
3. Gross margin is **≥60%** after including API, software, contractor, and fulfillment costs.
4. Fulfillment difficulty is **≤2/5** and delivery is repeatable.
5. Fulfillment time is **≤4 hours per customer**, with a plan to reduce it.
6. Evidence quality is **≥4/5**.
7. Distribution traction is **≥3/5**, with at least one repeatable acquisition source.
8. The next dollar spent has a measurable purpose and expected payback.
9. No scale spend exceeds **$40** without human approval.

Scaling means repeating a proven sale or delivery motion—not building more features by default.

# HUMAN APPROVAL GATES

Human approval is required before:

- Any spend above **$20** on a new experiment.
- Moving from research to prototype.
- Moving from prototype to validation.
- Any recurring software/API subscription.
- Any single purchase above **$25**.
- Any experiment exceeding **$60 total spend**.
- Using contingency/reserve funds.
- Hiring contractors or giving external parties customer data.
- Continuing after a kill rule is triggered.
- Changing price, target customer, or offer in a way that materially changes the hypothesis.

The approval request must state:

1. Spend requested.
2. Evidence collected so far.
3. Expected learning or revenue outcome.
4. Kill condition.
5. Remaining total runway.

# MANAGER DECISION RULE

Use this sequence:

### 1. Apply hard gates first

- **Payment, positive gross profit, feasible fulfillment:** eligible for scale.
- **Qualified interest but no payment:** continue only with one tightly scoped conversion test.
- **No meaningful interest, weak evidence, or expensive fulfillment:** kill.
- **Any budget breach:** pause until approved.

### 2. Calculate the experiment score

\[
\text{Experiment Score} =
2(\text{Revenue Evidence}) +
1.5(\text{Demand Evidence}) +
1(\text{Distribution}) +
1(\text{Gross Profit}) -
1.5(\text{Fulfillment Difficulty}) -
(\text{Spend}/\text{Stage Cap})
\]

Rate each positive category from 0–5:

- **Revenue Evidence:** payment and repeat purchase
- **Demand Evidence:** qualified conversations and buying signals
- **Distribution:** reply rate and repeatable access
- **Gross Profit:** margin and delivery economics
- **Fulfillment Difficulty:** 0 = easy, 5 = very difficult

### 3. Decision thresholds

- **Scale:** Score **≥12**, payment evidence exists, and all scale rules pass.
- **Continue:** Score **7–11.9**, with at least one qualified buying signal and no hard kill rule.
- **Kill:** Score **<7**, zero meaningful buying signals after the required sample, or any hard kill rule.

### Runway protection rule

Always preserve at least **$80 of the $200 total budget** until an experiment has produced payment evidence. Before payment, no experiment may spend more than **$60 total**, and total committed spend across all experiments may not exceed **$120**. The remaining **$80 is protected reserve** and requires explicit human approval.
