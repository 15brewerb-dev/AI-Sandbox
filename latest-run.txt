=== OFFER BUILDER RESULT ===

## 1. Offer name

**Columbus Plumber Lead Page — Live in 48 Hours**

## 2. Who it is for

Independent plumbers and small plumbing companies in Columbus, Ohio that:

- Have no website or an outdated one
- Depend mainly on referrals, Google Maps, or Facebook
- Want more phone calls and quote requests
- Serve a defined local area
- Can respond promptly to new leads

## 3. The exact problem it solves

When a potential customer searches for a plumber, they often cannot quickly see:

- What services the plumber offers
- Which areas they serve
- How to call or request a quote
- Why they should choose that business

The offer gives the plumber one clear, mobile-friendly page designed to turn local search traffic and referrals into phone calls and quote requests.

## 4. Deliverables

A completed lead-capture website delivered within 48 hours of receiving the required information:

- One mobile-friendly landing page
- Business name, phone number, service area, and hours
- Click-to-call button
- Quote-request form
- Service sections, such as:
  - Emergency plumbing
  - Drain cleaning
  - Water heater repair
  - Leak repair
  - Sewer or pipe services
- Short “Why choose us” section
- Frequently asked questions
- Basic local SEO title and description
- Form notifications sent to the owner’s email
- Basic thank-you message after form submission
- One revision round
- Simple instructions for updating contact details

Optional: connect the page to the customer’s existing domain or provide a low-cost temporary web address.

## 5. Price

### Founding-client price: **$497 one time**

Optional ongoing support:

- **$49/month** for hosting, minor text changes, form checks, and basic maintenance

The customer can also host it independently after delivery if preferred.

## 6. Why the price makes sense

The customer is not buying “a website.” They are buying a simple way for existing interest to become a call or quote request.

One additional plumbing job may cover the entire project cost. The fixed price is affordable for a small local business while covering:

- Copywriting
- Page design
- Mobile setup
- Form configuration
- Publishing
- Revisions
- Basic technical setup

The limited scope keeps fulfillment fast and avoids the cost of a traditional multi-page website.

## 7. Simple guarantee or risk-reversal

**48-hour delivery promise:** Once the business provides its logo, contact details, service list, and preferred service areas, the first version will be ready within 48 hours or the customer receives $50 off.

Also include:

**Approval-based payment:** The page is built as a preview first. The customer pays the remaining balance before it is published.

This avoids promising a specific number of leads or calls.

1. Pick one verified Columbus plumbing prospect with no website or a weak online presence.
2. Use a simple builder such as Carrd, Framer, Wix, or WordPress.
3. Create one page with:

5. Publish it on a temporary URL.
